## Why this page exists

Our work involves receipts, invoices, statements and correspondence — your business's
financial records, and sometimes your customers' details. If you are a bookkeeper or an
accounting firm, that data is also your clients' data and your obligation.

So this page is more specific than a privacy policy. It describes what actually happens
to your information when we build something for you.

The [Privacy Policy](/privacy) covers the website and the enquiry process.
This page covers a paid engagement.

## The short version

- Your data stays in systems you own wherever possible
- We take the minimum needed to build the thing, and no more
- We do not use your business data to train AI models
- When the engagement ends, we remove our access

## Where your data actually lives

We build inside the tools you already use — your spreadsheet, your accounting software,
your drive. The finished system reads and writes to your accounts, not to a database we
control. There is no ArkSystems platform holding your records, and nothing to migrate
away from if you stop working with us.

[CONFIRM: this is the current architecture for the expense-capture product. If any
engagement stores data on ArkSystems-controlled infrastructure — even temporarily, even a
queue or a log — that must be described here honestly rather than omitted.]

## What we access, and for how long

To build a system we need access to the accounts it will work with. We ask for:

- The narrowest permission that lets the job work, rather than full administrative
  access, wherever the tool allows it
- Access for the duration of the build and any agreed support period
- Removal of that access when it is no longer needed

## Sample data and demonstrations

We do not use your records in demonstrations, marketing, or on this website. Every figure
in the illustrations on our site is invented for the purpose.

If we ever want to reference work done for you, we ask first and in writing, and you can
say no without it affecting anything.

## AI processing

The systems we build use AI models to read documents and extract information. That means
your documents are sent to a model provider for processing.

## Confidentiality

We treat everything we see during an engagement as confidential. Where you need it in
writing — and accounting firms usually do — we sign your confidentiality agreement or
provide ours before any access is granted.

## If something goes wrong

If we become aware of a breach affecting your information we will tell you promptly,
describe what happened and what we are doing, and support any notification you need to
make to your own clients or to a regulator.

[CONFIRM: Canadian federal privacy law requires reporting breaches that pose a real risk
of significant harm to the Privacy Commissioner and to affected individuals, and requires
keeping a record of all breaches. Confirm you have a way to do this before publishing a
commitment to it.]

## When we finish

At the end of an engagement:

- We remove our access to your systems
- We delete working copies of your data from our machines
- The system we built keeps running in your accounts, under your control
