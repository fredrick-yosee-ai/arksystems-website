## What we collect, and why

**When you book a call.** Booking runs on Cal.com. When you book, Cal.com collects your
name, email address, time zone, and anything you type into the booking form, and shares
it with us so we can hold the meeting. We use it to contact you about that meeting and
any follow-up you have asked for. Cal.com processes this on its own infrastructure under
its own privacy policy.

**When you email us.** We keep the correspondence so we can answer you and refer back to
what was discussed.

**When you complete the AI Opportunity Fit Form.** We collect what you enter, including
information about your business and its workflows, and use it to decide whether we can
help and to prepare for a conversation. [CONFIRM: which form provider is used, and
whether responses land in email, a spreadsheet, or a form platform.]

**When you visit the site.** [CONFIRM: analytics provider — this section cannot be
finalised until that decision is made.] We record aggregate usage — pages visited,
approximate region, and which buttons are clicked — so we can tell which parts of the
site are useful. We do not use this to identify individuals.

Our web host, Netlify, records standard server logs including IP addresses, as any web
host does, for security and delivery. [CONFIRM: Netlify plan and log retention period.]

**What we do not do.** We do not sell personal information. We do not share it with
advertisers. We do not use it to build profiles for marketing to you across other sites.
We do not run advertising trackers or social media pixels on this site. Our fonts are
served from our own server rather than a font network, so simply loading a page does not
send your visit to a third party.

## Consent, and email

We rely on your consent, given when you book a call, send us an email, or complete a
form. Under Canada's anti-spam legislation we send commercial email only where we have
consent or an existing business relationship, and every such message identifies us and
offers a way to unsubscribe. You can withdraw consent at any time by replying or
emailing hello@arksystems.ca; withdrawing it does not affect anything we have already
done lawfully.

## Your rights

Under Canadian federal privacy law and British Columbia's provincial legislation you can
ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it if it is wrong,
and ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it. Email
hello@arksystems.ca and we will respond within [CONFIRM: 30 days is the statutory
expectation].

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled a privacy question, you can raise it
with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or with the Office of the
Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia.
