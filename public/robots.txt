# arksystems.ca
# Crawling is open. The three legal pages are kept out of search results by a
# `noindex` meta tag on the pages themselves, NOT by a Disallow rule here — and that
# distinction is load-bearing rather than stylistic.
#
# A Disallow would stop a crawler fetching the page, which means it never reads the
# noindex. Google can then still list the URL, with no title and no description,
# purely on the strength of links pointing at it. Blocking the crawl is how a page
# you meant to hide ends up in results as a bare URL.
#
# So: let crawlers in, and let the tag on each page do the work it was written for.

User-agent: *
Allow: /

Sitemap: https://arksystems.ca/sitemap-index.xml
