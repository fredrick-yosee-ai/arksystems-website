**What this page is, and is not.** These are terms for using the website. They are not the client contract. The engagement agreement — master services agreement, statement of work, or whatever the signed document is called — governs paid work, and it must take precedence over this page wherever the two touch. Section 10 says so explicitly, and that section is the reason this page is safe to publish before the engagement agreement is finalized.

**The commercially important section is 3.** ArkSystems publishes two figures — from CAD $1,000 and from CAD $4,000. Section 3 is what keeps a published figure from being treated as an offer capable of acceptance. It is the one part of this page worth a lawyer's specific attention.

## Notes for the reviewer

**The register.** Legal wording that counsel requires stays exactly as counsel writes it. The explanatory passages — sections 1, 2, 3 and 4 in particular — are written in plain English deliberately, because the same reader who reads the accounting page reads this one, and a page that suddenly reads like a different company undoes the work every other page does. Where a lawyer's wording is genuinely required, take it. Where it isn't, keep the plain version.

**Section 3 exists because the site publishes prices.** Most small-firm website terms don't need it. This one does, and it should not be trimmed as boilerplate.

**Section 10 exists so this page can ship first.** The engagement agreement is not finished. Section 10 means publishing these terms now creates no conflict with whatever that document eventually says.
